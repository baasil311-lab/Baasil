A letter to Leo

DEAR LEO,

I don’t know if these words will ever reach you, but I still want to write them.

Thank you.

Thank you for every goal, every assist, every impossible dribble, every moment that made millions of people fall in love with football all over again.

You didn’t just make me want to watch football.

You made me want to play it.

You became part of my childhood without ever knowing me.

I grew up watching you do things with a football that didn’t seem possible.

There were matches I watched just because I knew something special might happen when the ball reached your feet.

There were goals I replayed again and again.

There were moments that made me stop thinking about everything else and just watch.

Looking back, I realize it wasn’t only about football.

You made my childhood more beautiful.

You gave me something to look forward to.

You gave me moments I could remember.

And somewhere along the way, watching you became part of growing up.

When I step onto a football field, I carry a small part of the mentality you showed the world:

keep going,
keep believing,
and never stop chasing the next level.

You showed me that greatness isn’t only about talent.

It’s about dedication.

Patience.

Failure.

Sacrifice.

And continuing when people doubt you.

There were years when Argentina suffered heartbreak.

There were moments when people questioned you.

There were moments when the pressure seemed impossible.

But you kept coming back.

And eventually, you gave Argentina the moments they had been waiting for.

That is something I will never forget.

And now, seeing your international chapter come to an end honestly hurts.

A part of me wishes I could see you wearing that Argentina shirt forever.

I wish there could always be another World Cup.

Another final.

Another magical night.

Another moment where the whole world stops to watch you play.

But maybe that is what makes the story so meaningful.

You gave everything.

You didn’t just leave memories on football pitches.

You left them in people’s lives.

Mine included.

As I build my own future — through football, gaming, AI, and projects like JARVIS — I want to carry that same dedication with me.

I may never become Messi.

I don’t need to.

I just want to become the best version of Baasil that I can possibly become.

And if one day I ever get the chance to stand on a football field with you, I won’t need some huge speech.

I’ll probably just say:

Thank you, Leo.

Thank you for giving me something to dream about.

Thank you for making football feel like magic.

Thank you for making my childhood a little more beautiful.

Thank you for showing me what dedication looks like.

Your international journey may be ending,

but what you gave people like me isn’t ending.

The memories stay.

The inspiration stays.

The dream stays.

GRACIAS, LEO.
For every dream you gave us.
